# Age Verification

This check now runs through **Managed by Valyd** — [Verify & save a proof](/verifications/managed).

Age verification is no longer a self-serve direct call. Run it on a signed-in user's hosted
verification session: the age bands are computed from the account's KYC-verified date of birth and
returned to your app as a proof, while the raw DOB stays encrypted with Valyd.

The `POST /api/v2/age-verification` endpoint still exists in the
[Verification OpenAPI](/verifications/api) for existing integrations.
